Privacy Policy

Ikaystores · Effective 21 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Ikaystores marketplace app and website ("Ikaystores", "we", "us") collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to buyers, vendors, and visitors.

Information we collect

Account information. Your first and last name, email address, optional phone number, and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.

Delivery details. The delivery addresses and phone numbers you save, so vendors can deliver your orders.

Orders and payments. Your cart, order history, and payment status. Online payments are processed by our payment partners (Flutterwave and OPay). We never see or store your full card or bank login details. We keep only the payment reference, amount, and result.

Wallet and referrals. Your in-app wallet balance and transactions, your referral code, and which account referred you, if any.

Identity verification (optional for buyers, required for vendors). If you verify your NIN or BVN, the number is checked in real time with our verification partner (Dojah). We keep only the last four digits, the result, and the name on record. We do not store your full ID number, date of birth, or other details from the lookup. Vendors may also complete a selfie liveness check through Dojah. We store only whether it passed.

Vendor information. Business name, description and logo, payout bank account details, business registration and government ID documents uploaded for review, product listings and photos, and messages exchanged with the Ikaystores team.

Photos you upload. Product photos, logos, and documents you choose from your photo library or take with your camera. The app accesses your camera and photo library only when you choose to upload.

Information that stays on your device. If you turn on fingerprint or Face ID unlock, your device's operating system handles it. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Your sign-in session is kept in your device's secure storage.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or microphone audio, and we do not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To process orders, payments, deliveries, refunds, wallet credits, and vendor payouts.
  • To verify identity, prevent fraud, and keep the marketplace safe.
  • To send you order updates, account emails, and in-app notifications.
  • To provide customer and vendor support.
  • To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Vendors receive the name, delivery address, and phone number on your order so they can fulfil it.
  • Payment processors (Flutterwave, OPay) process payments and payouts.
  • Identity verification (Dojah) checks NIN/BVN numbers and liveness.
  • Service providers that host our servers, database, uploaded images (Cloudinary), and email delivery. They process data only on our behalf.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Access to personal data is limited to staff who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information. We keep order, payment, and payout records for as long as tax and financial laws require. See Delete your account for details.

Your rights and choices

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can also object to certain processing or withdraw consent. You can update most details in the app, and you can request account deletion at any time. To make any other request, email us at the address below.

Children

Ikaystores is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app or by email.
