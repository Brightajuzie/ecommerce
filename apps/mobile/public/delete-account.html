Delete your Ikaystores account

Ikaystores · Applies to the Ikaystores app and website

You can ask us to delete your Ikaystores account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This works for buyer and vendor accounts, including accounts created with Google sign-in.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ikaydigitalservices@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Ikaystores account".
  2. We may reply to confirm the request is really from you.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Email a deletion request

In the app, you can also get here from Profile → Delete my account.

What we delete

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and password
  • Your Google sign-in link, if you used one
  • Saved delivery addresses and your cart
  • Identity and liveness verification records
  • Notifications, referral details, and messages with our team
  • For vendors: business profile, logo, product listings and photos, payout account details, and uploaded verification documents

What we keep, and for how long

Some records must be kept for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, and legal reasons, even after your account is deleted:

  • Order, payment, refund, wallet, and payout transaction records, kept only as long as Nigerian tax and financial regulations require (typically up to 6 years). Then they are deleted.

These records are no longer linked to an active account and are not used for any other purpose.

Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.
  • Any unused wallet balance is lost. Vendors should withdraw their balance first.
  • Orders that are still open must be completed or cancelled before the account can be deleted.
